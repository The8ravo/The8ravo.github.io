About

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of California, Irvine, advised by Roman Vershynin.

Previously, I received my bachelor's and master's degrees from the University of Science and Technology of China, where I was advised by Dang-Zheng Liu.

My research interests are probability theory and random matrix theory, especially spectral statistics and universality for inhomogeneous random matrices.
