About

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of California, Irvine, advised by Roman Vershynin.

My research interests are high-dimensional probability and random matrix theory, especially spectral statistics for non-mean-field random matrices.

I received my bachelor's and master's degrees from the University of Science and Technology of China, where I was advised by Dang-Zheng Liu.
