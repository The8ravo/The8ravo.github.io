Ph.D. Student · Mathematics

Guangyi Zou 邹广翼

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of California, Irvine, advised by Professor Roman Vershynin.

Before coming to UCI, I received both my bachelor's and master's degrees from the University of Science and Technology of China, where I was advised by Professor Dang-Zheng Liu.

My research interests lie broadly in probability theory, with a particular focus on random matrix theory. I am also interested in number theory and enjoy writing expository notes on topics encountered in my studies.
